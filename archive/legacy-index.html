Common questions

What to know before choosing.

What exactly do I receive?

A free starter pack contains 25 standalone prompts for one task area. A paid system contains 60–75+ prompts in a defined order, plus input setup, a worked example, refinement prompts and a completion checklist. The Vault contains all eight systems and all eight starter packs, along with the stated examples and templates.

How is it delivered?

Email delivery is not connected in this front-end demo, so the page lets you preview three prompts but does not collect your email or claim to send the full pack. Paid delivery details must be confirmed before checkout is enabled.

What is the difference between a free pack and a paid system?

A starter pack is a curated set of prompts for one task area. A system adds a running order, the inputs each step needs, a worked output, a correction loop and a final checklist.

Is this a one-time payment?

The stated prices are one-time prices: $4.99 USD for one system, $9.99 USD for any three, and $19.99 USD for the Vault. Checkout is shown only when a corresponding payment URL is configured.

Which AI tools work with the prompts?

The prompts are written for conversational AI tools. Output can vary by tool, model and version, which is why paid systems include refinement steps.

Can I use generated outputs commercially?

The intended licence and commercial-output terms have not yet been published. Review the configured terms before purchasing once sales open; the page does not invent rights that have not been confirmed.

What happens after purchase?

Checkout and paid fulfilment are not connected in the current configuration. Once configured, the page can hand the selected product to Stripe, but the delivery method and support process still need confirmed business terms.
